Delete Your StandPoint Forestry Account

Account and associated-data deletion instructions.

← Return to StandPoint Forestry

StandPoint Forestry allows users to permanently delete their account and associated application data.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete Your Account From the App

If you can access your StandPoint Forestry account:

  1. Sign in to StandPoint Forestry.
  2. Open the My Stands screen.
  3. Select Delete My Account.
  4. Review the deletion warning.
  5. Confirm Delete Account.

Data Deleted With Your Account

When account deletion is successfully completed, StandPoint Forestry is designed to delete:

  • Your StandPoint Forestry account
  • Your account email association
  • Cloud-synchronized stand records associated with your account
  • Cloud-synchronized plot records associated with your account
  • Associated forestry plot measurements stored with those records
  • Associated plot GPS coordinates stored with those records

StandPoint Forestry will also attempt to remove locally stored stand and plot records associated with the signed-in account from the device performing the deletion.

Previously Exported Files

Files you previously exported or shared outside StandPoint Forestry, such as CSV or PDF reports, are not automatically deleted from locations where you separately saved or shared them.

You are responsible for deleting exported files from email, cloud-storage accounts, file folders, or other applications where you saved or shared them.

If You No Longer Have Access to the App

If you cannot access StandPoint Forestry but want to request deletion of your account and associated data, email the address below.

Email:
standpointforestry@gmail.com

Use this subject:
StandPoint Forestry Account Deletion Request

Send the request from the email address associated with the StandPoint Forestry account whenever possible.

For account security, additional verification may be required before a manual deletion request is completed.

Do not send your password. StandPoint Forestry support does not need your password to process or verify an account-deletion request.

Deletion Processing

In-app deletion is designed to begin immediately after you confirm the request. Manual deletion requests will be reviewed after the account holder's identity is reasonably verified.

If technical, security, fraud-prevention, or legal obligations require additional time or limited retention of specific records, the requester will be informed when applicable.
