StandPoint Forestry Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the StandPoint Forestry mobile application.

← Return to StandPoint Forestry
Effective date: November 18, 2026

StandPoint Forestry respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the StandPoint Forestry mobile application collects, why the information is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

StandPoint Forestry currently provides the Thinning Plot Tracker, a field tool designed to record and analyze forestry thinning plots.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create a StandPoint Forestry account, we collect the email address associated with your account.

Your account is used to identify you, authenticate access to the application, and keep your forestry data separate from data belonging to other users.

Forestry Field Data

StandPoint Forestry allows you to create and store forestry information including:

  • Stand names and identifiers
  • Property identifiers
  • Plot numbers
  • Tree counts
  • Average tree diameter (DBH)
  • Basal area calculations
  • Trees-per-acre calculations
  • Date and time associated with field records

This information is entered or generated as part of your use of the Thinning Plot Tracker.

Location Information

StandPoint Forestry can collect precise GPS coordinates when you capture the location of a thinning plot.

GPS information may include:

  • Latitude
  • Longitude
  • GPS accuracy

Location information is used to associate forestry measurements with their field locations and to display collected plots on the application's plot map.

StandPoint Forestry does not use your GPS location for advertising or behavioral tracking.

How We Use Information

Information collected through StandPoint Forestry is used to:

  • Provide the Thinning Plot Tracker
  • Save stands and forestry plots
  • Calculate basal area and trees per acre
  • Display plot locations on maps
  • Create stand summaries
  • Generate CSV and PDF reports
  • Synchronize your information between local device storage and cloud storage
  • Restore your information when you sign in on a supported device
  • Maintain and secure your StandPoint Forestry account

Offline Storage

StandPoint Forestry is designed for forestry field work where cellular service may be limited.

Stand and plot information may therefore be stored locally on your device using an offline database. This allows field data to be collected and retained without an active internet connection.

When cloud synchronization is available, records may also be synchronized with the StandPoint Forestry cloud database.

Cloud Storage and Authentication

StandPoint Forestry uses Supabase services for account authentication and cloud data storage.

Cloud records are associated with the authenticated user's account. Database access controls are used to restrict users to their own StandPoint Forestry records.

Maps

StandPoint Forestry uses mapping services to display GPS plot locations.

On Android devices, map functionality may use Google Maps Platform. On Apple devices, map functionality may use the mapping services provided by the operating system or supported mapping libraries.

StandPoint Forestry's plot map uses the GPS coordinates associated with your forestry plots to display their field locations.

Reports and Exports

You may choose to export your forestry information as CSV or PDF files.

When you choose to share an exported file, the operating system's normal sharing options may allow you to send the file to applications or services you select, such as email, file storage, or other applications.

StandPoint Forestry does not control how a third-party application you select handles information after you intentionally share an exported file with that application.

Sale of Personal Information

StandPoint Forestry does not sell your personal information or forestry field data.

StandPoint Forestry does not use your forestry field data for targeted advertising.

Data Retention

Your account and synchronized forestry data are retained while your StandPoint Forestry account remains active unless deletion is requested or required.

Local offline records may remain on a device until deleted through the application, removed during account deletion, or removed when application data is erased from the device.

Account and Data Deletion

You may delete your StandPoint Forestry account from within the application.

From the My Stands screen, select Delete My Account and confirm the deletion request.

Deleting your account is intended to permanently delete your StandPoint Forestry account and associated cloud stand and plot records.

For additional account-deletion information or if you no longer have access to the StandPoint Forestry application, see the StandPoint Forestry Account Deletion page.

Children's Privacy

StandPoint Forestry is a professional forestry field tool and is not directed toward children.

Security

StandPoint Forestry uses authentication, database access controls, and other technical safeguards intended to protect user information.

No system can guarantee absolute security, and users should protect account passwords and the devices on which StandPoint Forestry is installed.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as StandPoint Forestry develops new features or changes how information is processed.

When material changes are made, the effective date at the top of this policy will be updated.
